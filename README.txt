Add a virtual solar panel to Homey Energy for your balcony solar system.

Enter the current production in watts and the total generated energy in kilowatt-hours in the device settings. You can also display data from third-party solar equipment that has no dedicated Homey app. Use a script to retrieve the readings and pass them as number tokens to the two Homey Flow actions. The virtual solar panel then shows the current production and cumulative energy in Homey Energy.
